How to import [ SILLYTAVERN ]

THE CARD

  1. Download the PNG. Do not edit, resize or re-save it, because the card data lives inside the image.
  2. In SillyTavern, open the character panel and use the import button, then pick the PNG. Dragging the PNG into the window also works.

THE LOREBOOK

  1. Open World Info and use Import World Info with the _ST.json file.
  2. Attach it to the card (character lorebook) or enable it globally.
  3. Tag Lior vampire and Benny cannibal. Those tags are what open the vampire-only and cannibal-only entries for them.

Entries are keyword-triggered. If nothing fires, check that your global World Info scan depth is not set to 0.

JANITOR AI

  • Use the _JAI.json file. It only keeps the fields Janitor AI reads.
  • Please be nice to the models. They are doing their best.
